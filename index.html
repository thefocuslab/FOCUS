About FOCUS

A community built around consistent progress and meaningful mentorship.

Our purpose

FOCUS exists to help people turn ambitions into real, measurable progress. We believe that clarity of goals, consistent habits, and the right guidance can help anyone develop the discipline to achieve what matters to them.

We bring together members who want to grow — academically, personally, and physically — and connect them with mentors who are invested in their success.

Our three pillars

  • Goals: Every member sets goals in Academic, Personal, or Fitness. Goals have milestones, deadlines, and action plans that make them achievable.
  • Progress: Regular check-ins and progress updates create a record of what you've done, what worked, and what needs to change.
  • Mentorship: Mentors review your goals and progress and provide personalized guidance, feedback, and accountability.

Our values

  • Consistency over intensity — small steps, sustained over time, beat big bursts of effort.
  • Honesty over performance — an honest check-in about a difficult week is worth more than a polished one that hides the truth.
  • Privacy by default — your journal is yours. You decide what your mentor sees.
  • No judgment for setbacks — a missed milestone is a reason to review, not a reason to give up.

Who FOCUS is for

FOCUS is for students, young professionals, and anyone who wants a structured way to pursue their goals with support.

How FOCUS works

From signing up to achieving your goals — here's the full journey.

1. Join and create your account

Visit the Join page and choose your account type. Members sign up freely. Mentors and admins need an access code from the FOCUS administrators.

2. Complete your profile

Add a preferred name, a short bio if you'd like, and your preferred check-in day. You can update this any time in Settings.

3. Set your goals (optional at first)

You can set one main goal and up to two smaller goals. Each belongs to Academic, Personal, or Fitness. You can skip this and return later.

4. Break it into milestones

Add milestones to each goal — concrete steps that make a big target feel manageable. Ticking them off moves your progress bar.

5. Track your progress

Whenever you make progress, record it: what you did, what went well, what was difficult, and what's next. The platform builds a history of your development over time.

6. Complete your weekly check-in

Once a week, answer a short set of questions. Check-ins keep you and your mentor aligned, and create an honest record you can look back on.

7. Your mentor steps in

Your assigned mentor can see your goals, progress updates, and check-ins. They'll send recommendations, give feedback, and message you directly. You choose which journal entries to share with them.

8. Monthly reflection

Once a month, take stock of how things went. Monthly reflections are private to you.

9. Grow and adjust

Goals change. FOCUS lets you pause, edit, or complete goals as your circumstances evolve, while keeping a history of your progress.
